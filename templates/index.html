EXPLAINABLE LOGIC ENGINE

Multi-Factor Risk Assessment Engine

Deterministic & Transparent

1. Personalized Baseline Comparison

Generic clinical thresholds often miss subtle patient-specific deterioration. VitalGuard AI evaluates each vital against the individual patient's personal baseline range, calculating deviations proportional to distance from target.

2. Dynamic Trend Analysis

Analyzes historical trajectories over sliding 5-reading windows to identify steady rises, drops, or volatility (e.g. rising heart rate coupled with progressive desaturation) before single thresholds are breached.

3. Multi-Vital Correlation Matrix

Acute physiological distress rarely affects a single parameter. If tachycardia occurs alongside hypoxia and elevated temperature, a compounding cross-vital correlation penalty is triggered with clear clinical attribution.

HACKATHON ARCHITECTURE & ROADMAP

VitalGuard AI – Technical Overview

🏗️ Zero-Cost Hackathon Architecture

Designed for ₹0 cost, 100% offline demonstrability, and rapid 24-hour evaluation:

  • Backend: Python Flask lightweight microservice with SQLite database.
  • AI Risk Engine: Deterministic explainable multi-factor engine comparing personal baselines, multi-vital correlations, and rolling trends.
  • Frontend: Modern responsive dark healthcare dashboard with Chart.js visualization.

🚀 Future Scope & Hardware Integration

Planned production capabilities beyond hackathon simulation:

  • ESP32 Microcontroller: Direct IoT WiFi/MQTT telemetry streaming.
  • MAX30102 Optical Sensor: Real optical photoplethysmography (PPG) for Heart Rate and SpO2.
  • Medical Temperature Sensor: High-precision contact thermistor integration.
  • Bluetooth LE Wearable: Continuous ambulatory patient monitoring wristband.
  • Hospital EHR Integration: HL7 / FHIR protocol compliance and secure patient authentication.
